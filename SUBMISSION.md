Title: Gift Brief Match

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable public gift-brief marketplace. Proposers submit one declared gift, GenLayer consensus labels its fit against frozen preferences and hard constraints, a flagged proposal may be revised once, the organizer shortlists only FIT entries, and the named recipient chooses. It avoids private-trait inference, payments, and automatic purchasing; all selection authority stays with the humans named on-chain.
